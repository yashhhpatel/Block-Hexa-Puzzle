Hexa Fill Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Summary

Hexa Fill Puzzle is an offline puzzle game. It has no accounts and we do not run any servers that collect your data. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob and sells an optional “Remove Ads” subscription through Google Play; those Google services process some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress: levels, stars, coins, streaks, quests, settings and your chosen language.
  • Whether your Remove Ads subscription is active, so ads stay off while you are offline.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not subscribed to Remove Ads, the game shows banner, full-screen and app-open ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional Remove Ads subscription is sold and billed by Google Play. Your payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see them. The game only receives a purchase record from Google Play to check that your subscription is active. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Contacting us

If you email us through Settings › Contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
